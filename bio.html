Biography

Current work

Ruichen Xu is a Ph.D. candidate in Computational Applied Mathematics at Stony Brook University. The research program includes LLMs and agents for AI4Science, generative AI for physics- and constraint-aware sampling, and neural operators for partial observations and inverse problems.

Current projects study joint-embedding predictive learning for scientific data, sparse full-field PDE inference, partial-observation benchmarks, and LLM-guided molecular optimization, supported by reproducible training and evaluation pipelines and large-scale experiments on SLURM/CUDA high-performance computing clusters.
