Research agenda

Research focus

I develop reproducible learning systems for scientific data, with emphasis on sparse observations, structured representations, and reliable large-scale evaluation.

01 · Representation

Predictive learning for scientific data

Joint-embedding predictive architectures for PDE fields and multi-resolution graphs, including student-teacher training and latent full-field prediction.

  • JEPA
  • Graph learning
  • Neural operators
02 · Inference

Partial observations and inverse PDEs

Methods and benchmarks for sparse, irregular, sensor-style, and masked observations, with consistent datasets, configurations, and evaluation metrics.

  • JENO
  • PDEBench
  • Inverse problems
03 · Generation

Physics-aware generation and search

Diffusion, simulated annealing, and LLM-guided optimization for physical systems and molecular design under validity and multi-objective constraints.

  • Diffusion
  • Optimization
  • Molecules
