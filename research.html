Research

Learning from incomplete scientific data.

My work combines representation learning, neural operators, generative models, and optimization to recover structure from partial observations and enforce scientific constraints.

Research agenda

Core themes

Representation

JEPA for scientific data

Predictive latent learning for PDE fields and graphs, including multi-resolution partitioning and student-teacher optimization.

Inference

Neural operators under partial observation

Full-field reconstruction from sparse, irregular, sensor-style, or masked inputs with robust cross-resolution evaluation.

Generation

Physics- and constraint-aware sampling

Diffusion, simulated annealing, and LLM-guided search for PDEs, molecular structures, and multi-objective design.

Current work

Projects

Joint-Embedding Predictive Learning for Scientific Data

Develops JENO for sparse-observation PDE inference and HP-JEPA for multi-resolution graph representation learning, supported by reproducible student-teacher/EMA training, ablation, and GPU-cluster evaluation pipelines.

Period
2026–Present
Methods
JEPA · Neural operators · Graph learning
Stack
Python · PyTorch · SLURM/CUDA

PartialObs–PDEBench & AI4Physics

A curated AI4PDE/AI4SDE collection and benchmark for sparse, irregular, sensor-style, and masked observations, with unified datasets, configurations, and metrics for neural operators, diffusion models, and physics-aware baselines.

ORACLE: LLM-Guided Molecular Optimization

Combines LLM-proposed molecular edits with simulated annealing for validity-constrained, multi-objective structure-based drug design. Evaluation covers docking, QED, SA-score, diversity, and Pareto trade-offs through reproducible ablations.

Period
2025–Present
Methods
LLMs · Simulated annealing
