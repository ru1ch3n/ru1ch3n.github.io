Teaching & mentoring

From mathematical ideas to confident practice.

My teaching spans applied mathematics, numerical methods, probability, statistics, and computing. In research mentoring, I emphasize clear questions, reproducible experiments, and communication that makes technical work legible.

Teaching record

Instructor and teaching assistant at Stony Brook University since 2022; recitation leader and grader at NYU Courant from 2020 to 2022.

Approach

How I teach

01

Build intuition first

Connect formal methods to a concrete model, visual pattern, or computational experiment before adding abstraction.

02

Make reasoning visible

Show intermediate decisions, failure modes, and diagnostic checks so students can transfer a method to new problems.

03

Practice reproducibility

Treat code, configurations, results, and written explanations as one coherent piece of technical work.
